Computational physics

N-body solar system simulation

A 2D Newtonian simulation of the Sun and eight planets in Python, with an interactive animation (zoom, pan, lock the camera to any celestial body) and four simulated experiments built on top of it.

  • Three classic numerical integration schemes (Beeman, Euler-Cromer, direct Euler) compared by how well each conserves total energy over long runs
  • Simulated orbital periods of all eight planets measured and checked against NASA values
  • Satellite launches from Earth swept over speed and angle to find the trajectory that gets closest to Mars, and whether it makes its return journey sucessfully
